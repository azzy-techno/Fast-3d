# FAST Tower — an interactive visit to FAST O&M

A visitor arrives on a Dammam street at golden hour, walks into reception, meets a FAST engineer and
takes the lift to any floor of a concept building. Each floor is one discipline shown as a working
exhibit; the roof carries a cooling tower that opens layer by layer, and the visit ends over the city
at dusk. Everything a client needs — every service, its official page and how to contact FAST — is
also one tap away from the header at any time.

Live: https://azzy-techno.github.io/Fast-3d/ · Company site: https://fast-arabia.com/

Useful URLs: `/#electrical` (or any floor id) opens that floor directly; `?view=list` opens the quick
overview; `?lang=ar` starts in Arabic; `?probe` exposes the engine and frame times on `window.__fast`.

## What the visitor can do

- **Walk in**: wheel, swipe or arrow keys move the camera along an authored path that rests at every stop.
  Cars stop at the crossing while you walk over; the lobby doors open as you approach.
- **Choose any floor, any time**: the lift button (or `L`) opens the selector; the Services menu in the
  header jumps straight to a floor or to its official page; deep links work.
- **Follow the engineer**: he walks ahead at a human pace, stands clear of the text and points at the
  component being discussed. Component labels are real text and return you to that stop when clicked.
- **Inspect**: Level 01 opens the About viewer on the real certificates, supplier-portal logos, partners
  and the project list. Every service floor has its full official scope, real project names and photos.
- **Leave from the roof**: after the last cooling-tower stop the camera lifts off into a drone pull-out of the
  building. The lift button stays available for anyone who wants another floor.
- **A spoken guide**: with sound on, the engineer welcomes the visitor once at the reception desk, prompts at
  the lift, introduces each floor the first time its doors open, and says goodbye late in the closing drone
  shot. Script, timing and voice direction are in `src/voice.ts` (browser speech voices, English and Arabic).
- **Skip all of it**: the quick overview (also the default for reduced-motion visitors and for devices
  without WebGL) has every service, credential and contact on one ordinary, fast page.
- English and Arabic throughout, with mirrored layouts and the guide changing side with the text card.

## How it is built

| Part | Where | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Interface | `src/ui`, `src/state.ts`, `src/i18n.ts` | Preact + signals. Loads first (about 50 KB gzipped with all copy). |
| Content | `src/content` | Bilingual service, company and credential data. Every claim is sourced; see the file headers. |
| Engine | `src/engine` | Plain three.js, loaded lazily. Camera rail, virtual scroll, lift transitions, guide, labels. |
| Scenes | `src/engine/scenes` | `arrival` (street + reception), one `FloorScene` for levels 01–08, `roof`. |
| 3D assets | `tools/blender/*.py` → `public/models`, `public/tex` | Every model is generated by a script and its lighting is baked in Blender (Cycles). |

### Rendering approach

There are **no real-time lights or shadow maps**. Lighting is path-traced once in Blender and stored as
lightmaps (architecture) or vertex colours (equipment); at runtime the shader multiplies it with the
material and adds reflections from a pre-filtered panorama of the same space. That is what makes the
picture believable on an integrated GPU at 60–120 fps. Moving things (the engineer, cars, pedestrians)
use a small analytic sky / sun rig and soft contact shadows under each shoe.

Other choices that follow from profiling the previous version:

- The first view is a rendered still with the headline and navigation, usable in well under a second;
  the 3D scene replaces it when its first frame is complete.
- Each scene is a few hundred kilobytes; the next floor is downloaded and its shaders compiled in the
  background while the visitor looks around, and the lift doors only open on a finished frame.
- Scroll is virtual (wheel, touch and keys feed one spring-damped position), so pace is identical on
  every device and a fast wheel cannot throw the visitor past an exhibit.
- Resolution adapts to measured frame time; if a device still struggles, the visitor is offered the
  quick overview.

### Regenerating assets

Requires Blender 5.x (`blender` path is set in `tools/bake.sh`) and the two Poly Haven HDRIs in
`../_downloads/hdri` (see `docs/assets.md`).

```bash
bash tools/bake.sh sky city floor ex_electrical arrival roof   # any subset, or `npm run assets` for all
```

Each script accepts `--preview` (quick Cycles stills of every camera stop into `../_previews`) and
`--nobake`, which makes composing an exhibit a seconds-long loop. `tools/pack.mjs` then quantises and
meshopt-compresses the export.

## Honest limits

FAST Tower is a concept building made to show services; it is not a real address, and the district is
not a survey of Dammam. Screens in the exhibits are schematic and labelled as demos. See
`docs/design.md` for the direction and `docs/performance.md` for measurements and what was not verified.
