# About FAST gallery — level 12

The landing immediately below the cooling-tower roof opens into a 66 × 80 m exhibition hall at elevation 76 m. It has a complete coffered ceiling, timber ribs, warm lighting, limestone floor, bronze detailing, seating and a central globe installation. The original projection foyer remains at the entrance. Both lift shaft openings stay clear.

Thirty-three numbered displays form a guided circuit: three ISO certificates, LCGPA and Salamah, fifteen selected approvals/registrations, then thirteen technology partners. Certificates hang in illuminated frames; partner panels show the original logos and capability labels. Registration panels show the corresponding organization logos from page 5 of the supplied company profile, alongside the existing relationship labels. The same logos appear in the approvals projection. The duplicate ISO certificate wall behind the entrance hologram has been removed; the five dedicated certificate/license stops remain. Each display has a lectern, individual presentation framing and an active spotlight.

The engineer walks beside the visitor, stops at every display, points at the actual panel and presents with his free hand. The circuit follows the perimeter before visiting the four central partner islands and returning through a clear aisle to the existing lift. Stationary viewing intervals let visitors read without camera drift. Next, Previous, category shortcuts and a grouped display selector provide alternatives to scrolling. The About FAST lift destination lands at progress 1.535; the guided route occupies 1.53–1.74. Dedicated scroll anchors allocate more distance to the long hall while preserving the roof and lift timing.

Four entrance buttons open About FAST, Certifications, Approvals & registrations, or Technology partners. Physical partner displays open the matching partner in the holographic projection. This native modal dialog uses translucent cyan styling, a restrained opening animation and orbit graphic. The original thirteen partner logos appear in a searchable collection with bilingual capabilities, countries and official website links. Viewers can open the ISO PDFs, follow public credential links and expand the additional supplier registrations.

The header Partners action travels to the gallery behind closed lift doors before opening the projection. Visitors can close it and resume the tour or choose another floor. Projection content scrolls independently while the tour is locked. Escape closes it, focus returns to the opener, and arrow/Home/End keys operate its tabs. Reduced motion disables decorative animation. List view provides the same source content without WebGL. English and Arabic tour controls work on desktop and phone.

## Validation

`BASE_PATH=/Fast-3d/ npm run build` checks TypeScript and the production deployment base. `npm run test:about` samples 50,001 positions across the hall, checks camera/guide continuity and separation, every display footprint, all 33 presentation holds, and scroll/progress round trips. `npm run test:journey` covers the complete route; `node scripts/travel-motion-test.mjs` checks lift and traffic choreography.

`npm run test:about:hall` visits all 33 rendered stops, checking pointing alignment, supporting-hand animation, camera clearance, selected content, reverse/direct navigation, partner projection, phone layout, Arabic, WCAG A/AA and travel onward to Electrical. Captures are saved in `artifacts/about-hall/`. `node scripts/about-browser-test.mjs` covers the original projection interactions, certificate links and list fallback; `ABOUT_LIST_ONLY=1` selects the content-only pass. Software-rendered checks do not benchmark hardware frame rates.
